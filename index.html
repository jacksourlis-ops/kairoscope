FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
WHAT IS KAIROSCOPE?
Kairoscope is an AI-powered horoscope and dream interpretation app. "Kairos" is the ancient Greek concept of the perfect, sacred moment in time. We help you see yours.
HOW ARE READINGS GENERATED?
Every reading is uniquely written by Claude AI for your sign, the current moon phase, and today's date. No pre-written horoscopes — every reading is one of a kind.
WHY CAN'T I GET A NEW READING TODAY?
Your moment is set by the cosmos — not chosen by you. Once your reading is revealed it stays fixed for that period. This prevents cherry-picking and keeps your reading authentic. Return tomorrow for a fresh daily reading.
IS MY DATA PRIVATE?
Yes. Your dream journal and birth chart are stored only on your device — never on our servers. We are a Canadian company under PIPEDA. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your information to train AI.
WHAT IS PRO?
Pro unlocks unlimited readings, your full birth chart (sun, moon and rising calculated with professional astronomical precision), personalised natal chart readings, unlimited dream journal, pattern analysis, and weekly cosmic forecasts. $4.99/month, cancel anytime.
HOW ACCURATE IS THE BIRTH CHART?
Very accurate. We use Jean Meeus astronomical algorithms — the same foundation used by professional astrology software — to calculate your exact sun, moon and rising signs.
WHAT DO THE MOON PHASE THEMES MEAN?
Kairoscope's visual theme shifts automatically with the real moon phase — darker near the New Moon, most luminous at the Full Moon — keeping you connected to the actual cosmic moment.
HOW DO I CANCEL MY SUBSCRIPTION?
Cancel anytime from your account settings or by emailing hello@kairoscope.app. Access continues until the end of your billing period.
