FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
WHAT IS KAIROSCOPE?
Kairos is an ancient Greek word for the perfect, sacred moment in time. Not clock time, but the right time. The moment when everything aligns. Kairoscope helps you see that moment through your stars and your dreams.
HOW ARE READINGS CREATED?
Each reading is calculated fresh using live astronomical data, the current moon phase, planetary positions, and your personal chart. No templates, no recycled content. Your reading is unique to you and to this exact moment in time.
WHY CAN'T I GET A NEW READING TODAY?
Your moment is set by the cosmos, not chosen by you. Once your reading is revealed it stays fixed for that period. Come back tomorrow for a fresh daily reading. The stars do not change their mind because you refresh the page.
IS MY DATA PRIVATE?
Your dream journal and birth chart live only on your device. Nothing personal is stored on our servers. We are Canadian, we operate under PIPEDA, and privacy is something we take seriously. No ads, no data selling, no exceptions.
WHAT DOES PRO INCLUDE?
Pro gives you unlimited readings across all three modes, your complete birth chart with sun, moon and rising sign calculated using professional-grade astronomical precision, readings personalised to your natal placements, an unlimited dream journal, pattern analysis across your entries, and weekly cosmic forecasts. $4.99 a month. Cancel any time.
HOW ACCURATE IS THE BIRTH CHART?
We calculate your chart using Jean Meeus astronomical algorithms, which are the same mathematical foundations used by professional astrology software worldwide. Your sun sign is exact. Your moon sign is accurate to the day. Your rising sign requires your birth time and city for full precision.
WHY DOES THE APP LOOK DIFFERENT SOMETIMES?
Kairoscope reads the actual moon phase and shifts its visual theme accordingly. Near the New Moon the app goes deep and dark. At the Full Moon it reaches peak luminosity. It is a small way of keeping you connected to the real sky above you.
WHAT ARE THE AMBIENT SOUNDS?
The ambient sound is wind chimes with a subtle cosmic resonance underneath. It is designed to deepen the reading experience without distracting from it. You can turn it on or off any time using the button at the top of the app.
HOW DO I CANCEL?
Cancel any time from your account settings. Your Pro access runs until the end of your billing period. If you ever have trouble, email hello@kairoscope.app and we will sort it out personally.
